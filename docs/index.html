02 / Research & development

Academic work.

Three projects at the intersection of mechanism theory, simulation, and robotics.

CSIR-CMERI / B.Tech. thesis / 2022

Underactuated
hand exoskeleton

Analysed a five-digit, six-actuator system for master-slave grasping and force-feedback applications. MATLAB kinematics and MSC Adams simulation guided linkage optimization and exposed an index-finger collision limit.

16-link, 3-DOF finger mechanisms · 110° MCP flexion

Original schematic / not a lab drawing

IIT Delhi Robotics Lab / 2020–22

Mechanisms,
made interactive

Extended MechAnalyzer with 11 interactive 3D models, including gear trains, straight-line mechanisms, and six- and eight-bar linkages. Added modules for involute gear meshing and the history of mechanism theory.

C# · OpenGL · Coupler curves · Kinematic analysis

Original mechanism illustration

INAE / CSIR-CMERI / 2021

Parallel-kinematic
system modelling

Derived a six-state model of an ultra-precision coordinate measuring stage and characterised its response under four vertical and lateral load combinations. Separately designed an LQR controller for a cart-pendulum benchmark.

State-space modelling · MATLAB/Simulink · MSC Adams
